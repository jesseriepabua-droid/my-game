# EGG SHOCKERS 3D — Timed Matches (3/4/5 Min), Cumulative Kill Scoreboard & Final Podium Rankings

## New Features Added
1. **Selectable Match Timer (3, 4, or 5 Minutes)**: Choose **3 Minutes**, **4 Minutes**, or **5 Minutes** on the main menu before starting an offline or online Game Code match. A live countdown clock displays at the top center of the screen and pulses red in the final 15 seconds.
2. **Cumulative Kill Scoreboard (Persists Across Deaths)**: Dying and respawning **never resets your kills**—every player's and AI bot's eliminations accumulate throughout the entire match.
3. **End-of-Match Winner Screen & Full Rankings**: When the timer hits `00:00`, the **Final Match Standings** screen displays the **#1 Champion** at the top followed by every player ranked from **2nd place down to the lowest kill count**.
